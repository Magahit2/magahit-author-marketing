# Magahit Author Marketing

BUILD A PREMIUM AUTHOR MARKETING AGENCY WEBSITE

Create a complete, premium, modern, conversion-focused website for Magahit Author Marketing, an author marketing and book growth agency.

The website should feel like a high-end author growth consultancy, not a generic freelance marketing website.

The primary objective is to make authors immediately think:

“These people understand books, readers, discoverability, author branding, and long-term growth. I can trust them with my book.”

The website must be visually impressive, highly professional, trustworthy, responsive, fast, easy to navigate, and designed to convert visitors into qualified author leads.

1. BRAND

Brand Name

Magahit Author Marketing

Brand Positioning

Magahit Author Marketing helps authors increase their book's discoverability, reach the right readers, improve their book's marketing assets, build their author brand, and create sustainable audience-growth systems.

Do NOT position the company as merely a “book promotion service.”

Position it as:

An author growth and book marketing agency.

The website should communicate three major pillars:

DISCOVERY

Get the book in front of more of the right readers.

CONVERSION

Turn interested readers into buyers.

AUDIENCE

Build an audience that can continue supporting the author's current and future books.

Use this framework throughout the website:

Discover → Attract → Convert → Retain → Grow
